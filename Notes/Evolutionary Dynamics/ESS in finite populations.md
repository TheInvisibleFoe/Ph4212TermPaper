Suppose the Cooperator defector PD game with the payoff matrix
$$
M = 	\begin{bmatrix}
a & b \\
c & d
\end{bmatrix}
$$
Then the redefined ESS for finite populations a strategy B is an $ESS_{N}$ is defined using the following two conditions,
- **Selection opposes A invading B**: 
  $$
  	F_{1} < G_{1} \implies b(N-1) < c+ d(N-2)
  $$
- **Selection opposes A replacing B**: 
  $$
  	\rho_{A} < \frac{1}{N} \implies a(N-1) + b(2N-1) < c(N+1) + d(2N-4)
  $$

**This makes a VERY IMPORTANT point, If a strategy is ESS or not also depends on the population size.**
